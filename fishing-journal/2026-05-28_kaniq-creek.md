## Lure-Weight Gap Insight
Kaniq's common-salmon spoon is the **1/2 Oz Medium Spoon #3/0** — but this is **below** the Loki's lure-weight band (5/7 – 2 1/3 Oz). The **JigWinner 8'10" SE** light spinning rod throws it cleanly but gets **overpowered by larger fish**. There's a gear gap.

**Fix:** The Loki CAN throw the 1/2 Oz spoon (Kaniq's river is narrow — short casts still hit the sweet spots), but the clean fix is to pair the Loki with the **Club-Series Flat Spoon 1 1/2 Oz #6/0** — sits dead-center in the lure band, fishes the same retrieve cadence, and turns the Loki into a true competition rig. Keep the JigWinner + 1/2 Oz Medium Spoon as the common-salmon volume rig.

## Black Needle Retrieve Technique (CRITICAL — different from Loki)
The Black Needle uses a **fundamentally different retrieve** from the slot 3 Loki rig:

1. **Drop to bottom** — let the 1.5 Oz Flat Spoon settle on the bottom first
2. **Slow-and-go** cadence with **medium-fast reel speed**
3. **Pulse the rod** to keep the lure **bouncing off the bottom** — it lifts briefly into the water, then drops right back
4. **NOT dragging** the bottom — bouncing it
5. **Repeatedly and very quickly** — rapid pulsing cadence, much faster than the Loki's drop-and-pause
6. **Constant movement** at maximum depth — the lure stays glued near the bottom
7. **Prevent the lure from leaping up** into shallower depths — monster-unique strike zone is tight to the bottom

**Why this matters:** A lure that climbs through the water column leaves the strike zone. Monster uniques hold near the bottom and commit only when the lure is in their face, pulsing aggressively without breaking depth. The Loki's slower drop-and-pause cadence walks the lure through too much water column to consistently hook this fish class.

**Conditions:** Needs **deep water** — find the deepest available water near the marker and keep the lure as close to the bottom as possible throughout the retrieve.

## Zone Geography (where the fish classes live)
Kaniq isn't uniform — there are **three distinct fish-class zones** based on water depth and spot location, not just "Kaniq is salmon water."

| **Trophy/small unique** | "Angling the Middle-Earth" → open water facing Hunter's Cabin | Shallower | 20-40 lb trophy + small unique | **Loki + Mono .016"** (slot 3) |

**Critical insight:** The monster-unique deep zone is a SPECIFIC stretch — starting at Hunter's Cabin and working toward the middle island. **Estimated 2-3 monster-unique spots in this section.** Don't try to fish for monsters at Angling the Middle-Earth — those are shallower zones and hold the smaller unique class.

**Validated catches:**
- **60 lb unique** — landed on Loki (just barely; the bigger one in the next spot snapped it)
- **70 lb unique** — landed on Black Needle, but the rig was STRESSED. The 50 Lb fluoro ceiling was working hard.

**Tackle ceiling consideration:** If user wants to land the 80-90 lb apex class consistently, the Black Needle is borderline. A heavier rod + heavier line/leader would give more margin. Worth investigating the next step up (e.g., Wicked Viking or similar X-Heavy spinning).

## Pike-Leader Tradeoff (Black Needle)
Northern Pike are present at Kaniq, so titanium leaders are the standard recommendation. The Black Needle monster-unique rig uses **fluoro leader (not titanium)** because leader stretch matters more than pike protection when fighting a 70 lb salmon. Accept the occasional pike bite-off as the cost of landing monster uniques.
