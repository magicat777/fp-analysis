## Key Findings

### Casting beat spinning for lure placement
- **Slot 3 (SharpCaster casting) vs Slot 2 (Salmonster spinning)** for trout spinners
- Casting rod let me **place the spinner in the sweet spot** with better precision
- Spinning rod caused the lure to **raise and drop too much**  -  inconsistent depth presentation
- **All 4 trophy-class trout (Trophy Cutthroats + Rainbows) came off the SharpCaster**

### SharpCaster + Counsellor 3500 P SE validated
- Confirmed the shallow/light reel pairing works well for trout-class fish
- Trophy Cutthroats 2.77 and 3.21 lb landed cleanly on 1/2 Oz spinner, #3/0 hook
- Drag slipping at 23 Lb was appropriate for 2-3 lb class fish  -  no torn hooks

### Bite quality was the limiting factor
- **Soft and fast bites**  -  many hits, hard to set the hook in time
- Barbless #3/0 hook requires immediate, aggressive hookset on the strike
- Next time: try smaller hook (#2/0 or #1/0) for faster hook penetration on light strikes

### Rod/lure weight mismatch still present
- SharpCaster rated 5/8 - 2 Oz; ran 1/2 Oz (under minimum)
- Casting was still effective but range suffered  -  could have thrown further with a Light power rod
- **For next trout comp:** try **JigWinner 8'10" SE (Light, 1/6-5/8 Oz)** paired with Counsellor 3500 P SE for proper rod loading + extended casting range
