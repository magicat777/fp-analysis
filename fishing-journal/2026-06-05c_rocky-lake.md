## Headline
**Dual telescopic sequence tactic confirmed.** Cast TeleValentine first (12" / #4 / mayflies); when it hits, immediately follow up the EXACT SAME SPOT with the TeleDragon (22" / #2 / maggots). **~50% of the time a second fish commits within ~15 seconds** on the follow-up. Active panfish zones are STRATIFIED across both depth layers — a hit at 12" is a strong signal that 22" is also live at the same spot.

## The Sequence Tactic
1. **Cast TeleValentine first** (shallow, 12", mayflies). The shallow layer is the "scout" — faster bite signal because surface-active fish commit first.
2. **If TeleValentine hits**, immediately cast TeleDragon to the **exact same spot** (deep, 22", maggots).
3. **~50% hit rate on the follow-up cast** within **~15 seconds** of dropping the maggot.
4. If TeleValentine doesn't hit by ~30s, move on — the zone isn't active. Don't waste a follow-up cast on a cold spot.

## Why This Works
- **Panfish stratify at productive zones.** A hit at 12" doesn't mean the 12" layer is the ONLY active layer — it means the SPOT is active across the column.
- **The shallow layer hits faster** (surface-active fish commit first), so it functions as a free "is this spot live?" scout cast.
- **The deep layer commits in the same 15-second window** when the spot is hot, which is squarely inside the light/medium fish wait-time target (15-30 sec per [[feedback_competition_tactics]] stopwatch protocol).
- **Effective catch-rate lift: ~50% on confirmed-hot zones.** If half the productive spots double up, that's a meaningful multiplier on a 30-minute count comp.

## Tactical Implications for Next Comp
- **Always cast the TeleValentine (12" / mayflies) FIRST** at each new spot. It's the diagnostic.
- **Keep the TeleDragon (22" / maggots) staged** for immediate follow-up — bait pre-hooked, ready to swing out.
- **Skip cold spots fast.** If the TeleValentine hasn't hit by ~30 sec, swap zones — don't sink a follow-up cast.
- **The 15-second follow-up window** is the operational constraint: cast the TeleDragon FAST after the TeleValentine hits, while the spot is still active. Hesitation costs the second fish.
- **Estimated leaderboard impact:** at the AM comp pace (19 fish in 30 min), a 50% follow-up multiplier could lift count to ~28-29 — meaningfully closer to the 40-50 top-4 ceiling without solving the wait-time discipline gap yet.
