**Verdict:** Worked fine for trout, but **30-45 second hits** — too slow for a 1-point/fish format. Trout were the target species, but the comp scoring punishes wait-time. Swapped to TeleDragon + maggots + shallow panfish for higher cycle rate.

## What Worked
- **Telescopic rod = no reel-in cycle** — set, yank, swing fish to hand, re-bait, re-cast. The fastest reset of any rod type for sport comps.
- **2 Lb mono on a 3-13 Lb rod handled fish 1-3 lb above the line rating** — telescopic technique (no drag, no reel) lets the rod bend absorb the load. Landed a 3.1 lb White Sucker on 2 Lb mono.
- **X-Series Slim Float + #2 hook + maggots at 22"** = ultralight strike detection for panfish (crappie, bluegill, golden shiner, white sucker mix).
- **Shallow fixed depth (22")** = fishing the surface-active panfish layer where bites are densest in the morning.

## What Didn't Work / Frustrations
- **30% off the leaderboard pace.** Top-4 are doing something materially better — likely one of:
  1. **Shorter wait time** before recasting (cut at 20 sec, not 45 sec)
  2. **Different depth** (deeper or shallower than 22")
  3. **Different bait** (worm/insect alternative giving faster strikes)
  4. **Different hook size** (smaller for faster commits)
- **Unique trout in the area takes 1.5-2.5 minutes to hit** — not worth a single point in this format, despite being the longest-fish path to 2nd-place secondary score.
- **OmniFloat trout rig was the wrong tool for sport format** — match rod cadence is too slow for 1pt/fish; reserved for catch-quality formats.

## Key Findings
1. **Telescopic rods are the sport-competition meta for high-cycle 1pt/fish formats.** No reeling = fastest fish-to-hand cycle. Set + yank + swing + reset.
2. **You can fish 1-3 lb over rated line on telescopic** because the rod blank absorbs the load — no drag mechanism to fail.
3. **Trophy 3.1 lb White Sucker on 2 Lb mono confirms the over-spec landing capability.**
4. **30-45 second hooksets at 22" depth on maggots = panfish baseline at Rocky.** Top competitors are likely cutting wait time or fishing a tighter depth.
5. **Unique fish targeting is incompatible with 1-pt/fish formats** — the 1.5-2.5 min hit time burns through too many potential count points.
