## ⚠ Critical Rig Constraint

**Three-way / Texas / Carolina rigs only work on SPINNING and CASTING rods.** They cannot be used on:
- Bottom rods (Humerus, Mokonzi, Tribal Totem)
- Feeder rods
- Carp rods
- Match rods (Brutus)

This means for Congo monster hunting with the three-way rig, only **Slot 2 (JigWinner spinning)**, **Slot 3 (SharpCaster casting)**, and **Slot 4 (NileChasseur spinning)** are rig-compatible. Bottom rods (Slots 5/6/7) must continue using traditional sinker + leader + bait rigs.
