## Diagnostic Tell  -  How to Spot a Monster Early

Confirmed today: **extra-big fish (300+ lb) never pause their aggression.** They pull continuously until you're out of line. This is the missing early-warning signal.

| Pull Behavior | Likely Class | Action |
|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Pulse-pull-pause-pulse (intermittent) | <100 lb | Standard retrieve |
| Strong pull with brief pauses | 100-300 lb | 100+ lb finesse protocol |
| **Continuous, no pauses** | **300+ lb** | **Extra-slack finesse IMMEDIATELY** |

**Why timing matters:**
- ID within 5 sec of hookup → ~5-10 min fight
- ID after 30+ sec of brute-forcing → 10-15+ min + reel wear + line risk
- Never ID → broken reel in ~100 sec

**The trick:** know you have a monster BEFORE too much line peels off. The earlier you transition to finesse, the shorter the fight and the less wear on tackle.
