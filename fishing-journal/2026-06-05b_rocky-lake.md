# Session: Rocky Lake — Telescopic Hypothesis Validation (Post-Comp Practice)
**Date:** 2026-06-05 (PM, ~1 hr after the AM "One by One" comp)
**Level:** 91
**Format:** Free fishing — practice to validate AM hypotheses

## Headline
**Hypothesis confirmed: this is a telescopic-only competition format.** OmniFloat 14'10" SE with **identical tackle** to the TeleValentine 23' was measurably **2x slower** in fish-cycle rate. Also validated multi-depth + multi-hook strategy — 44 fish in the practice session across 12" and 22" depth bands and #2 + #4 hooks on mayflies + maggots.

## Rigs Tested
### Rig 1 — TeleValentine 23' (telescopic, second validated chassis)
| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| Rod | **TeleValentine 23'** (Telescopic) |
| Reel | **Heartspinner 2500** |
| Line | Mono ~2 Lb |
| Hook | **#4** |
| Bait | Mayflies / Maggots |
| Depth | **12"** (NEW shallower band) |

**Result:** Targeted a **different panfish layer** at 12" than the 22" maggot layer. Bite cycle on par with the TeleDragon 23' AM rig.

### Rig 2 — TeleDragon 23' (AM comp MVP, revalidated)
- Continued producing at 22" with #2 hook + maggots — the AM baseline still holds for the deeper panfish band

### Rig 3 — OmniFloat 14'10" SE (match rod, with telescopic-equivalent tackle)
- **Same 2 Lb mono, same hook size, same bait, same depth** as the telescopic rigs
- **Cycle rate was ~2x slower** than the telescopic — measurable, not subjective
- **Reach advantage:** Got to a few **more distant spots** the telescopic couldn't reach
- **Verdict: not worth it.** The hit-to-hook duration is too long for 1pt/fish scoring. The extra reach doesn't compensate for the doubled cycle time.

## Session Result
- **44 fish** in the practice block (vs 19 in the 30-min AM comp)
- Multi-depth (12" + 22") + multi-hook (#2 + #4) + multi-bait (mayflies + maggots) all productive
- Confirms there are **at least two panfish strike layers** at Rocky — not one fixed depth

## Key Findings
1. **OmniFloat 14'10" SE = 2x slower than telescopic with identical tackle.** The bottleneck is the **rod-type cycle**, not the line/hook/bait/float/depth/reel. Match rods are out for this comp format.
2. **OmniFloat is the LIGHT match rod** in inventory — any heavier match would cycle even slower. **Conclusion: NO match rod is competitive at Rocky 1pt/fish.**
3. **TeleValentine 23' + Heartspinner 2500** = second validated telescopic chassis. Two rods now confirmed — gives a backup if one breaks down or is in repair.
4. **12" depth opened a separate panfish layer** from the 22" baseline. Multi-depth strategy is a real lever — fish whichever band is more active on the day.
5. **#4 hook works alongside #2** — hook size is a soft variable; pick whichever commits faster on the given bait.
6. **Reach trade-off is unfavorable** — distance casting matters less than cycle speed in count-optimization formats.

## Implication for Next Competition
- Run **two telescopics in parallel** — TeleDragon at 22" / #2 / maggots + TeleValentine at 12" / #4 / mayflies. Cover both panfish layers without rod swaps.
- **Skip the OmniFloat entirely** for sport comps at Rocky. Reserve it for trout-quality formats where selectivity and longer casts matter more than cycle rate.
- The **30% leaderboard gap** is still open. With multi-depth/multi-hook proven, the next variable to dial in is **wait-time cutoff** (test recasting at 20 sec vs 30 sec vs 45 sec).
