## Key Decision: Silverthorn → SharpCaster Swap

After this trip and reviewing prior Congo PM marathons, the Silverthorn Stake 7'6" has been retired.

**Why:**
- Line weight range 35.3 - 88 Lb is too narrow-high  -  good for 150+ lb fish, poor for anything under 50 lb
- Lure weight 7/8 - 3 1/6 Oz forces heavy lures only; can't fish lighter presentations
- At Congo, the rod was underpowered on bigger Nile Perch  -  fish would overwhelm the rod and force line-slacken rescues
- At other locations (Saint-Croix, SJD, Quanchkin, Kaniq), it was always over-speccified

**Replacement:** SharpCaster 7'3" + Rivertex Tornado 7500
- Line weight: 28.6 - 66 Lb (wider mid-class range)
- Lure weight: 5/8 - 2 Oz (casts lighter lures)
- Same X-Heavy power, Fast action, 7'3" shorter frame for better casting accuracy
