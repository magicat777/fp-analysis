## Headline
**New Neherrin MVP for light-to-medium panfish/bass/shad: JigWinner 8'10" SE + InspireCast 3000 SE (27.5" retrieve) + Mono .008" (4 Lb) + Casting Spoon 1/6 Oz #2.** Walking the river edge produced **43 fish, 46.174 lb keepnet** including **Unique White Crappie 2.635 lb** and **Trophy White Crappie 1.351 lb**. Also validated a **two-pass dual-rod coverage tactic** (TeleValentine match → JigWinner casting on the opposite/structured side) that the user projects at **40-60 fish per circuit** in non-comp or pure-quantity comp settings.

## Backstory: 6/9 Setup-Fail at the Neherrin Minimal Competition
- **Arrived an hour early** with the **TeleValentine match** (telescopic), planning to pre-scout panfish positions along the river edge. Marked the productive spots along the edge with the telescopic.
- **Comp rules were spinning/casting only** — telescopic disqualified.
- Tried to modify other light rods on the fly during the comp — couldn't dial in a panfish-productive lure setup before time ran out.
- **Lesson: VERIFY the comp rod-class FIRST.** Pre-scouting with a banned rod-class is wasted prep — you still know where the fish are, but you can't fish them with what was scouted. Read the rules block BEFORE traveling. See [[feedback_competition_tactics]] addition.

## Lure-Selection Journey (How I Got to the #2 Spoon)
1. **Jig + Twin Tail Spider Grub / Shad / Craw soft baits** — hit bass cleanly but **panfish hits were unreliable**. Decided to switch off jig.
2. **Slop #4 spoon** — hit bass but skipped panfish entirely. Wrong hook size for the target.
3. **Casting Spoon 1/6 Oz #2** — **unlocked panfish strikes** alongside the bass/shad bycatch. This is the working size for the Neherrin panfish strata.
4. **Result on the #2:** Couldn't crack Golden Shiner with it; only one (trophy-class) Bluegill; no Redear Sunfish. **White Crappie + American Shad** dominated the catch.

## Retrieve-Speed Finding — 27.5" Beats 31.5"
- First attempts ran a **31.5" retrieve reel** — produced decent results but felt **slightly too fast for panfish commit**.
- Swapped to the **InspireCast 3000 SE (27.5" retrieve)** — **hit on nearly every cast along the water's edge.**
- **Takeaway:** For Neherrin panfish on the #2 casting spoon, sub-30" retrieve reels are the working window. Faster reels skip the fish; slower reels would let them inspect-and-pass.

## Line Tradeoff — 2 Lb vs 4 Lb at Neherrin
- **2 Lb mono** would be sufficient for panfish alone (0.22-1.5 lb fish class).
- But the **#2 casting spoon catches bass and shad as bycatch**, which **overloads 2 Lb mono** to breakage.
- **4 Lb mono is the right tradeoff** — it stresses to **~97% on the larger shad and trophies** but holds with careful play. **No leader needed.**

## Structure Access (Where the JigWinner Beats the Telescopic)
- The casting setup **reached rocky and weedy areas the telescopic could not cover**.
- This is where the **new trophies + the unique White Crappie were sitting** — not on the open edge, but at structure adjacent to the panfish-dense edge.
- The telescopic was effective at the easy edge zones; the JigWinner is the only way into the structured zones that hold the size class.

## Dual-Rod Two-Pass Coverage Tactic (Non-Comp or Quantity-Comp Optimal)
For **non-competition fishing OR pure quantity/weight panfish comps where both rod classes are allowed:**

| Pass | Rod | Hook | Depth | Bait | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 (Lap 1)** | **TeleValentine** (match) | **#12** | **16"** | **Pearl Barley** | Panfish on the river edge — 5-20 sec hits, 20-30 fish per 20-min lap |
| **2 (Lap 2)** | **JigWinner 8'10" SE** | #2 (casting spoon body) | retrieve-depth | Casting Spoon 1/6 Oz #2 | Re-cover the OPPOSITE / structured side of the same productive zones — pulls **another 20-30 fish** (panfish + bass + shad mix) |

**Why two passes (not same-spot like Rocky):**
- **Different zone access:** The telescopic match maxes out at the close edge; the JigWinner reaches structure the telescopic can't. They cover DIFFERENT physical zones at the same productive areas.
- **Different prey response:** Pass 1 (bait) commits panfish without spooking the larger predators. Pass 2 (spinning lure) brings the predators (and remaining panfish willing to chase) into play.
- **Pearl barley filters bass/shad** in pass 1 — keeps the rate up on panfish without overloading the 2-3 Lb telescopic line on bycatch.
- **Casting spoon in pass 2 accepts the bycatch** — 4 Lb mono is sized for it.

**Sequence vs Rocky Lake's:** The **Rocky 6/5c dual-telescopic** tactic is **same-spot, depth-stratified** ([[feedback_telescopic_sport_competition]]). The **Neherrin two-pass** tactic is **same-zone, structure-stratified** with different rod CATEGORIES (match + spinning). Both are dual-rod strategies — see new [[feedback_dual_rod_two_pass_coverage]] for the generalized principle.

## Why This Wasn't the Comp Rig on 6/9
- The 6/9 minimal comp was **casting/spinning only.**
- User's pre-scout was done with the (banned) telescopic match.
- Even with the right rod-class understanding now, on a future Neherrin minimal comp the JigWinner + #2 spoon **is** the rig — but the **pearl-barley telescopic pass cannot be used in spinning/casting comps**. You lose pass 1; you keep pass 2.
- **In a Neherrin minimal comp specifically:** Run the JigWinner + #2 spoon, target structured areas first (where the trophy/unique fish sit), then sweep the edge for panfish count.
