## Key Findings

### ⭐ SharpCaster + CGT BBgun 4500 + Bullet Spinner 5/6 Oz #2/0 = MVP setup
- **Hit ratio: ~8/10 casts produced fish**
- **Retrieve:** slow stop-and-go at **2/3rds depth** (surface = 0/3, bottom = 3/3, target = top of bottom third)
- Fish targeted: **Brown Trout, Tiger Trout, Muskie, Tiger Muskie, Northern Pike, Smallmouth Bass**
- Landed **all 3 trout PBs + Unique Brown 12.255 lb** on this rig alone
- Titanium Leader .011" + Braid .0084"  -  good line match for mid-class predators

### CGT BBgun 4500 validation
- 44 Lb drag / 6.5:1 ratio  -  handled 12 lb Unique Brown Trout cleanly
- Better choice than Tornado 7500 for Saint-Croix mid-weight fish
- Confirms matrix recommendation (CGT BBgun 4500 = HEAVY tier for SharpCaster)

### Muskie/Pike slow morning  -  crankbait was the exception
- Tried multiple hook sizes, baits, locations for Muskie/Pike first thing
- Only **Crankbait 3 ft #3/0** on slot 4 (NileChasseur) produced consistently
- Attributed to weather (partly cloudy) or timing
- **Shiners** and other live baits produced slowly

### JigWinner 8'10" SE not used at Saint-Croix
- Equipped but not cast  -  Saint-Croix 5-6 lb trout stress a 20 lb setup easily
- Light power (4-12 lb line spec) under-gunned for this lake's fish class
- **Confirmed: JigWinner is a trout-comp-only swap**, not a Saint-Croix tool
- Swap JigWinner back to Salmonster (or similar Light-Heavy spinning) for non-comp Saint-Croix trips

### Bottom rigs (5/6/7) consistent Blue Cat producer
- Medium Cutbait + Duck Mussel Meat + Large Minnows  -  all worked for 25-45 lb Blue Cats
- Single 75.9 lb Unique Blue Cat on Large Minnows
