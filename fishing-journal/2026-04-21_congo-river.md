### Tackle
- **Rod:** NileChasseur 6'3" SE (Spinning, X-Heavy, 1-2 3/5 Oz lure, 46-97 Lb line)
- **Reel:** GiantSpin 9000 SE
- **Rig:** **Three-way rig**  -  main line → three-way swivel → dropper to sinker (bottom) → leader to hook (suspended)
- **Hook:** Offset Hook
- **Bait:** **Ribbed Shad 6"** soft plastic on the offset hook

### Why this rig changes everything

The three-way rig + offset hook + Ribbed Shad is fundamentally different from our previous Congo meta (Flat Spoon 1 1/2 Oz + active drop-and-lift on Silverthorn). Key differences:

1. **Suspended presentation**  -  Three-way rig keeps bait off the bottom in a natural hover zone; Nile Perch strike from below
2. **Soft-plastic profile**  -  Ribbed Shad has action even at rest (flow-driven); spoons require active retrieve
3. **Offset hook buried in soft plastic**  -  weedless presentation with solid hooksets
4. **NileChasseur handles the monster weight**  -  97 lb line class proved sufficient for 257 lb fish (2.6× rod rating)
