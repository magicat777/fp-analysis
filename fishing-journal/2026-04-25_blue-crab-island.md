## ⚠ Loadout Experiment  -  Mixed Results

Pre-trip configured Slots 2 and 5 with rods designed to fill mid-light gaps in the lure-weight spectrum. Goal: cover shallow (1/5 Oz jig) → medium (1/2 Oz lure) with optimal reel speed and lure placement.

### Verdict: 5/7 slots GREAT, 2/7 slots POOR

**WINNERS:**
- **Slot 1  -  Brutus 10'10" SE + CGT SpinTitan 4500** ⭐ "Perfect for this location"  -  right balance of strength + durability for medium-weight fish
- **Slot 3  -  SharpCaster 7'3" + VorteX 7000 SE + Glow&Holo Medium Spoon 1 Oz #4/0** ⭐ "Ideal"  -  covers full lure spectrum, placed 1 Oz spoons day + night
- **Slot 4  -  NileChasseur 6'3" SE + GiantSpin 9000 SE + Three-way + Ribbed Shad 6"**  -  Congo MVP rig transferred well; landed Unique Snook + Unique Gafftopsail
- **Slot 6  -  Mokonzi 12'10" SE + L'impulsion 12000 SE + Large Cutbait** ⭐ MVP  -  landed both biggest Uniques (Black Drum 82.786, Red Drum 66.827)
- **Slot 7  -  Tribal Totem 12'10" + Savanna Spirit 11000 + Shiners**  -  solid bottom workhorse

**LOSERS:**
- **Slot 2  -  UncleSam 6'3" Spinning + Patriot's Dream 4000** ❌ "Less than ideal"  -  reel speed wrong for the lures, didn't match jig sizes, insufficient line strength for medium-big fish. Reel wear: 27,226/46,200 (59%)
- **Slot 5  -  LibertyCast 8'10" Casting + AmericanEagle 2000** ❌ "Less than ideal"  -  too light for the larger fish. Reel wear: 9,242/46,200 (**20% RED  -  needs repair**)

### Key learning
**Undersized rod/reel + medium-big fish hits = significant reel wear.** Both DLC-acquired rods (UncleSam, LibertyCast) are not bad rods generally  -  they just don't match Blue Crab Island's fish class spread (Drum, Tarpon, Snook in the 25-80 lb class). The Brutus + SharpCaster combo handled the same fish without durability damage.

## Tactical Findings

### Boat dock = day + night productive
The Bass Base spot produced consistently throughout the day. **Night = more big fish + faster hit rates** off the boat dock  -  this is where the Unique Drum class hit on Mokonzi bottom + Large Cutbait.

### Bait pairings confirmed
- **Large Cutbait + bottom rig** → Drum class (3 Trophies + 2 Uniques on this combo alone)
- **Major Popper 1 Oz #3/0 + evening** → Bowfin + Longnose Gar (both Trophies)
- **Walker 1 Oz #3/0 + evening** → Butterfly Peacock Bass (Trophy)
- **Glow&Holo Medium Spoon 1 Oz #4/0 + day/night** → Striped Bass + Red Drum (Trophy)
- **Leeches + early morning** → Brown Bullhead (multiple Trophies)
- **Craw 4" + morning** → Unique Gafftopsail Catfish

### Night fishing window
First Premium-license advanced trip with night access. Confirmed: night-class big fish (Black Drum, Red Drum Uniques) hit reliably between **early night and night** time periods.
