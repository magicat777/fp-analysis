## The Validation Story

This was the maiden voyage for the **DragonCast 7'3" NE + Basilisk 3500 P** combo as the new Slot 1 mid-range casting workhorse (replacing the retired Brutus 10'10" SE match rod). The specific design goal was a rod that bridges the gap between:

- **Heavy Congo slot** (NileChasseur 6'3" SE  -  46-97 Lb line, monster-class)
- **Light trout-comp slot** (JigWinner 8'10" SE  -  4-12 Lb line, sub-5 lb fish)

DragonCast's 7.5-23 Lb line class lands squarely in the 5-10 lb bass/trout shallow-water sweet spot.

## Tactical Findings

### DragonCast + Basilisk 3500 P = ideal 5-10 lb rig
- Rod flex loads properly on JigHead 1/2 Oz (sits in the middle of 3/8 - 1 3/7 Oz range)
- Basilisk's 23 Lb drag slips cleanly on small bass/trout  -  no torn hooks
- 6.2:1 retrieve is balanced for both slow-roll and medium-speed presentations
- Titanium Leader .011" handles pike bite-off bycatch at pike-present lakes (White Moose, Neherrin)

### Twin Tail Spider Grub 2" is a universal mid-range bait
Worked at all 4 locations despite different target species (Largemouth, Smallmouth, Lake Trout, Brook Trout, Burbot). Confirms the "one rig for everything" concept from the Congo PM session (Ribbed Shad 6")  -  same principle, smaller scale.
