**USER-VALIDATED MVP (Kaniq + SJD + White Moose Lodge, 2026-05-16):**

| Slot | Spec |
|---|---|
| Rod | **Loki 7'6" SE** (spinning) |
| Reel | **EspiraDoublePunch 4500 SE** — 4.5:1 ratio, 24.2 Lb max drag, 27.5" recovery, 9+1 BB |
| Line | **Mono .016"** (~20 Lb test) |
| Leader | **Fluorocarbon Leader .015"** (~18 Lb) |
| Lure | **Medium Spoon 1 Oz #4/0** |

**Field results:**
- XP rating: **neutral to +2** on every catch (sweet-spot match per [[feedback_lure_balance_triangle]])
- First or second cast per spot = hookup
- Strike feedback: **medium to medium-heavy** — clear rod-tip telegraph, not subtle, not violent
- Species hit: largemouth bass, white bass, sockeye salmon, chinook salmon, **trophy trout** (multi-species spoon-chaser profile)

**Why this works:**
- 20 Lb mono is the lightest line that confidently lands a Chinook (20-30 lb adult class) without overpowering the 4-10 lb resident bass/trout
- 18 Lb fluoro leader is below the mainline (good break-priority) but heavy enough for salmonid abrasion at structure
- Medium Spoon 1 Oz at 27.5" recovery + 4.5:1 = the slow-flutter retrieve speed salmon and trophy trout respond to
- Spinning + mono (not braid) gives the line stretch that buffers salmon head-shakes — a known weakness of braid setups for chinook

**Where this beats the user's prior attempts:**
- The DLC "Irish" rod (28 Lb class) was hooking fish well but XP showed **three red arrows = -3 (overpowered)** — kills the lure-balance XP multiplier per [[feedback_lure_balance_triangle]]. Stepping down to Loki 7'6" SE returned XP to the matched band.
- The Irish rod also couldn't be saved by re-lining (still overpowered relative to fish weight class).

**How to apply:**
- **For Kaniq, SJD, White Moose:** recommend this as the **default Slot 1 salmonid/mid-bass rod** when the trip targets mixed-class freshwater species (4-30 lb).
- **For pure trout sub-5 lb work:** still prefer JigWinner 8'10" SE (matched lighter class).
- **For pure bass shallow-water work:** still prefer DragonCast 7'3" NE per [[feedback_midrange_slot_validation]] — the Loki is a salmonid-first rod with bass crossover, not the inverse.
- **Lure swaps that should still hit XP band:** Medium Spoon 3/4 Oz #2/0 (lighter water), Trophy variants of Medium Spoon if owned (per [[feedback_trophy_vs_xseries]] — open trophy first, fill with standard).
- Do NOT swap to braid — the mono stretch is part of why this hooks/holds salmon.
