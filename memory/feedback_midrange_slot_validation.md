The DragonCast 7'3" NE (Casting, Heavy, 3/8-1 3/7 Oz lure, 7.5-23 Lb line) paired with **Basilisk 3500 P (23 Lb drag, 6.2:1)** fills a specific role in the 7-rod loadout: **mid-range shallow-water casting for 5-10 lb bass and trout.**

**Why:** User specifically wanted a mid-range casting rod to bridge the gap between:
- Heavy Congo slot (NileChasseur 6'3" SE  -  46-97 Lb, monster-class)
- Light trout-comp slot (JigWinner 8'10" SE  -  4-12 Lb, sub-5 lb fish)

DragonCast sits at the 7.5-23 Lb line class  -  ideal for 5-10 lb bass and trout in shallow water.

**Validated 2026-04-21:** Single-day mission completion at Falcon Lake, Neherrin River, White Moose Lake, and Emerald Lake using this setup. Jighead 1/2 Oz #3/0 + Twin Tail Spider Grub 2" was the default terminal tackle.

**How to apply:**
- For any trip targeting Bass (Largemouth/Smallmouth), medium Trout, Walleye, or small Pike in shallow water, recommend Slot 1 DragonCast as the primary rod
- Default terminal: JigHead 1/2 Oz #3/0 + Twin Tail Spider Grub 2" (confirmed working)
- Alternate terminals for the rod's lure weight range (3/8-1 3/7 Oz):
  - Shallow crankbaits (bass reaction)
  - Spinnerbaits 3/8-1/2 Oz (bass + pike)
  - Topwater walkers or poppers (pre-dawn/dusk)
- Reel alternates for different trips: Counsellor 3500 P SE (bass cranks), AmericanEagle 2000 (topwater burn, 7.4:1)
- This rod is also rig-compatible  -  can deploy Three-way/Texas/Carolina rigs if shallow bottom targeting is needed
- Don't recommend for fish >15 lb  -  rod line rating maxes at 23 Lb, any heavy fish will overmatch it
