**CORRECTION 2026-04-26:** Buzzbaits in Fishing Planet are **NOT topwater lures** as commonly assumed in real-world fishing parlance. They are **spinner-type lures with a clacker and often a soft bait attached**, fished primarily in mid-water and along the bottom.

**At Blue Crab Island specifically (validated 2026-04-26):**
- **Lure used:** Buzzbait 1 Oz #5/0 + Larva 4" / Worm 5" attachment
- **Rod:** SharpCaster 7'3" + VorteX 7000 SE = MVP combo
- **Technique:**
  1. Cast and let lure sink to bottom
  2. **Bounce off the bottom** with a small lift
  3. **Raise for a few seconds** (mid-water hold)
  4. **Let it drop** back toward bottom
  5. **Bounce a bit off bottom** again
  6. Repeat the cycle
- **Hookset:** **PAUSE LONGER than usual before setting**  -  fast hookset snaps the lure out of the fish's mouth. Wait for clear weight commitment.

**Why this works:**
- Bottom-feeders (Drum, Bowfin, Catfish) strike on the drop phase
- The clacker/blade attracts fish to the area
- Soft bait attachment provides scent/texture commit
- Pause-before-hookset gives the fish time to fully take the lure

**How to apply:**
- For BCI: recommend Buzzbait 1 Oz #5/0 with Larva 4" or Worm 5" on SharpCaster as a high-producer combo (validated)
- For Saint-Croix Pike/Muskie: similar technique works (drop-and-pause near submerged structure)
- For ANY location with mid-water/bottom predators: buzzbait is a viable choice
- **Don't recommend buzzbait as a topwater lure**  -  that's wrong for FP mechanics
- Don't rush the hookset  -  count 1-2 seconds after the strike before setting

**Distinguishing from real-world buzzbait usage:**
- Real-world buzzbait = topwater chugger
- FP buzzbait = mid-water/bottom spinner with clacker
- Same name, different mechanics  -  don't conflate them when coaching technique

**Related techniques:**
- Same drop-and-pause logic as Congo three-way + Ribbed Shad 6"
- Same drop-and-pause logic as Kaniq Medium Spoon 6/7 Oz salmon retrieve
- All bottom-feeder hunts use variations of "bait reaches bottom → fish commits on drop, not raise"
