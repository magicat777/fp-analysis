On 2026-04-26 user discovered DragonCast 7'3" NE (Casting, Heavy, 3/8-1 3/7 Oz lure, 7.5-23 Lb line) is **NOT** suitable for Blue Crab Island despite seeming to work initially.

**What happened:**
- Hit rate at BCI was good  -  fish striking consistently
- Action was fast, retrieve rate was very good with paired Basilisk 3500 P reel
- BUT: broke several lines and lost tackle during fish fights
- Tried upgrading reel to **VorteX 7000 SE (33 Lb drag)**  -  overpowered for the rod, **still broke tackle**

**Why:** The **rod blank**, not the reel or line, is the limit. DragonCast's Heavy power class with 23 Lb max line rating is calibrated for **bass-class fish (5-15 lb in shallow water)**. At BCI's deeper water with 30-100+ lb fish (Drum, Snook, Tarpon), the rod can't sustain the load even when the reel/line theoretically can. Stronger reel doesn't fix a too-light rod.

**Where DragonCast IS the right rod (validated 2026-04-21):**
- Falcon Lake (Largemouth)
- Neherrin River (river bass)
- White Moose Lake (cold-water bass)
- Emerald Lake (Smallmouth/Largemouth)
- Saint-Croix Lake shallows (smaller bass)
- Other shallow-water 5-15 lb fish locations

**Where DragonCast is WRONG:**
- Blue Crab Island (any spot)
- Congo River (any class)
- SJD sturgeon
- Dnipro Wels Catfish targets
- Any saltwater or 30+ lb fish location

**How to apply:**
- For BCI Slot 2 replacement, recommend **Rivertex Salmonster 8'2"** (X-Heavy spinning, 5/8-2 Oz, 15-42 Lb line)  -  proper line capacity for BCI mid-class fish
- Don't suggest reel upgrades to "fix" DragonCast at BCI  -  the rod is the limit, not the reel
- For warm-water bass missions (Falcon/Neherrin/Emerald), DragonCast remains the correct pick
- This generalizes: **Heavy class rods with sub-25 Lb line ratings are bass-class, not multi-purpose**
