**Mechanical definition of "Straight" technique (FP):**
- Constant reel input at **speed +1 to +3** with NO twitch, jerk, or pause
- Any pause or rod-tip action retags as Stop&Go or Twitching → breaks "Straight" quest credit
- An initial "straight slow" segment as the lure picks up speed still counts as Straight (field-confirmed 2026-05-17 — quest credit registered on a hit during the slow-to-speed-2 ramp)
- Rod-tip position: hold still at mid-height; any twitch risks the Twitching tag

**Why:** Daily quests like "catch a common fish by straight retrieve" require this technique tag at strike time. Picking the wrong lure (jig, popper) means zero quest progress no matter how many fish you catch.

**How to apply (lure selection):**
- **Crankbaits at minimum speed** — most-cited community choice
- **Inline spinners / bullet spinners** at speed +2 to +3 (blade needs flow to spin)
- **Spinnertail soft plastics** on spinner heads — field-validated salmon-hitter (see below)
- **Casting spoons** — flutter on steady pull works
- **AVOID** for this quest: jigs, soft plastics on jigheads, topwater (poppers/walkers need rod action)
- Spinnerbaits are contested — some say works, others say Stop&Go is required

**How to apply (location for "common fish" quest):**
- **Emerald Lake (NY)** or **Mudwater River (MO)** with a small inline spinner along weed edges — bluegill / perch / crappie / pickerel will stack fast
- Any panfish-dense shallow shoreline works
- Hold rod still, reel steady +1 to +2

**Critical technique detail — "drop to bottom first":**
- Both validated catches above happened with the **lure allowed to sink near the bottom BEFORE starting the retrieve**
- This converts the rig into a **lift-from-bottom straight retrieve** — the spinner blade kicks on as the line tensions and the lure rises through the water column at speed +1→+2
- Why it works: bullet-spinner head + spinnertail body has enough weight (5/6 oz) to reach bottom quickly even in deeper marker zones; salmonids holding near bottom commit to the rising vibration
- Implication: **count down the sink** before reeling — don't start retrieve on splashdown. This is materially different from the panfish/inline-spinner technique where you start reeling immediately to keep the blade spinning shallow.
