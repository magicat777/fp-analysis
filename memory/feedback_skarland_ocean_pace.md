**Rule:** Skarland Fjord (and likely other deep-ocean locations) follow a **slow but high-yield rhythm**: 2-4 minutes between bites, 5-10 minute reel/fight time on big fish, ~4-8 catches per hour with 3 stationary E-rods + 1 active casting rod.

**Skarland is the fastest leveling location in the game** (user confirmed 2026-05-01 across multiple trips):

| Location | Avg time per level (real-time) |
|---|---|
| **Skarland Fjord** | **2-4 hours** |
| Congo River | 4-8 hours |
| Everywhere else | 6-16 hours |

When a user is grinding to a specific level (DLC unlock, license tier, gear gate), Skarland is the answer if their gear can survive the repair burn. Congo is the fallback when E-class gear isn't owned yet.

**Why:** Validated 2026-04-29 first trip. User leveled 56 → 57 → about-to-58 in ~2 hours real-time (~2x Congo's XP rate per real-hour) despite fewer total fish. Big fish hit at 500-900 ft depth; reeling roughly 1 minute per 100 ft of depth means a 600+ lb fish at 800 ft requires ~8 minutes just to bring boatside. Confirmed 2026-05-01 across additional trips: averaging 1 level / 2-4 hr is the steady-state pace, not a one-off.

**How to apply when coaching:**

1. **Set expectations for pace before recommending the trip.** Skarland is NOT Congo's chase-and-catch. Telling a user expecting Congo-style action that they'll get 4-8 catches/hour will be jarring without context.

2. **3 bottom E-rods + 1 casting rod = optimal deployment.** All three E-rod slots dropped to bottom on bait-and-wait. The casting rod handles cover/structure work between bottom-rod bites.

3. **Don't recommend the active retrieve playbook here.** Saltwater patience > freshwater activity. These fish are scent-feeding bottom predators, not chase-and-strike pelagics. Dead-stick at depth, then slow lift-and-drop every 30 sec is the cadence.

4. **XP/hour > fish/hour at Skarland.** A single Greenland Shark = 30,101 XP. A single Unique Atlantic Halibut = 100,868 XP. One trophy-class fish = a level-up. Coach the user to optimize for big-fish opportunities, not keepnet fill speed.

5. **Fight duration math:**
   - Cod/Ling (20-50 lb): 1-2 min reel from bottom-rod depth
   - Trophy/Unique mid-class (50-150 lb): 3-5 min
   - Shark/Halibut class (200-650 lb): 8-12 min, line-tension-aware
   - Greenland Shark / Halibut at 800+ ft: schedule it - other rods may bite while you're fighting

6. **"Letting them off" is an explicit tactic, not a failure.** When hooked too big for the setup, loosen line slack and set the rod in the holder until it tears off. This costs less than reel destruction. User confirmed this is what experienced ocean players do.
