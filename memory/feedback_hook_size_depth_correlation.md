---
name: Hook size + bait depth correlate to fish size at depth-fishing locations
description: At Skarland (and likely other deep-bottom locations), hook size and the depth at which bait is presented roughly correlate to the size class of fish caught
type: feedback
---

**Rule:** At deep ocean bottom-fishing locations, **bigger hook + deeper bait drop = bigger fish**. Smaller hooks at shallower depths produce filler-class catches; the largest tungsten hooks (#10/0, #12/0) at maximum drop depth produce trophy/unique-class targets.

**Why:** User observation 2026-04-29 first Skarland trip: "I found that the size of my hook and the depth of the bait roughly correlated to the size of the fish." Empirical pattern from a 24-fish session including Greenland Shark 636 lb, Unique Atlantic Halibut 409 lb, Porbeagles 193-262 lb.

**Validation data from Skarland session:**
- **Tungsten Golden #12/0 at deep drop (Slots 5-7 E-rods):** Greenland Shark 636 lb, Young Greenland 422/362 lb, Unique Halibut 409 lb, Porbeagle 262 lb, Young Porbeagle 193 lb, Unique Common Ling 71 lb
- **Triple Hook Pilker 7 Oz #8/0 at mid-depth:** filler Cod/Ling 20-30 lb
- **Smaller hooks at shallow drops:** Haddock 8 lb class

**How to apply when coaching:**

1. **Match hook to target class, not just bait size.** At deep ocean: #12/0 for shark/halibut hunting, #8-10/0 for cod/ling trophy work, #6/0 for haddock filler.

2. **Use depth as a fish-class filter.** If user wants to skip filler and hunt trophies, drop deeper (PulseWave/Chillarc max sinker drop). If user wants keepnet volume fast, fish shallower with smaller hooks.

3. **Recommend the Tungsten Golden Hook line** for endgame saltwater. Validated #12/0 at Skarland for the full range of large fish.

4. **Bait choice still matters above hook/depth.** Giant Tiger Prawn was the universal Skarland producer regardless of hook/depth combo. The hook+depth axis selects fish *size class within the bait's catchable species pool*, not species pool itself.

**Caveat:** This is one trip's pattern. The depth-vs-class relationship may flip at non-ocean locations (river current, lake structure) where fish behavior is different. Treat as Skarland/deep-saltwater-specific until validated elsewhere.
