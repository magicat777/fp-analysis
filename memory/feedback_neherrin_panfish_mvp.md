**Rule:** For Neherrin River free fishing AND for Neherrin spinning/casting-only competitions, the MVP rig for light-to-medium fish (panfish, American Shad, small-to-medium bass) is:

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| Rod | **JigWinner 8'10" SE** (Spinning, Light, Fast, 1/6-5/8 Oz, 3-13 Lb) |
| Reel | **InspireCast 3000 SE** (**27.5" retrieve — critical**) |
| Line | **Mono .008" (~4 Lb)** — no leader |
| Lure | **Casting Spoon 1/6 Oz., #2** |

**Why:** Field-validated 2026-06-10 at Neherrin River, level 93. Walked the river edge + structured zones (rocks, weeds) and pulled **43 fish, 46.174 lb keepnet** including **Unique White Crappie 2.635 lb (16.7")** and **Trophy White Crappie 1.351 lb (13.6")**, plus multiple American Shad in the 1.9-2.7 lb range. The JigWinner reaches rocky/weedy structure the TeleValentine match CANNOT cover — and that's where the trophy/unique fish sit. The Casting Spoon 1/6 Oz #2 is the lure size that admits panfish strikes (slop #4 skipped panfish and only caught bass; #2 hits both).

**Retrieve-speed dial-in (the non-obvious bit):**
- **27.5" retrieve (InspireCast 3000 SE) = hits on near-every cast.**
- **31.5" retrieve = slightly too fast — fewer panfish commits.**
- Sub-30" retrieve reels are the working window for Neherrin panfish on the #2 casting spoon. Faster reels skip the fish.

**Line tradeoff — why 4 Lb not 2 Lb:**
- 2 Lb would be enough for panfish alone (0.22-1.5 lb class).
- The #2 casting spoon catches **bass + American Shad as bycatch**, which **breaks 2 Lb mono**.
- 4 Lb mono stresses to **~97%** on the larger shad and trophies but holds with careful play. **No leader needed.** This is the right tradeoff for non-comp panfish mode; in pure panfish-count comps (if it exists for Neherrin), 2 Lb could be tested but bycatch risk remains.

**Alternate bait on same rig (bonus finding 6/10):**
- **Spawn Sack** on the JigWinner produced American Shad in the 1.9-2.0 lb range alongside the casting spoon. Useful when spoons run low or for still-bait variation on the same rod/reel/line.

**Species that DECLINED the #2 spoon at Neherrin 6/10:**
- Golden Shiner (none caught)
- Redear Sunfish (none caught)
- Bluegill (one trophy only — light hit rate)

White Crappie + American Shad + smaller bass dominated. If targeting the declined species specifically, test smaller lure sizes or alternate baits — open observation.

**How to apply:**
- **For Neherrin free fishing:** Run as Slot 1. Pair with TeleValentine match (#12 hook / 16" / pearl barley) in Slot 2 for [[feedback_dual_rod_two_pass_coverage]] two-pass strategy.
- **For Neherrin spinning/casting-only competition:** Run as the sole comp rig — the TeleValentine match is BANNED in those rulesets (see 6/9 setup-fail lesson in [[feedback_competition_tactics]]). Target structured zones first for trophy/unique pulls, then sweep the edge for panfish count.
- **Don't substitute DragonCast 7'3" NE** — DragonCast is rated 5-10 lb bass/trout class; the panfish here are too small for it to register fast strikes consistently. JigWinner's Light power is correct.
- **Don't substitute a faster-retrieve reel** — 27.5" is empirically the band. Stay sub-30" retrieve.
