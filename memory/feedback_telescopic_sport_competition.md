**Rule:** For **count-optimization sport competitions** (1 point per fish, short timer, insect/worm bait), default to a **telescopic rod + ultralight panfish rig** — NOT a match rod, NOT a spinning rod. The telescopic eliminates the reel-in cycle, which is the rate-limiting step on a 30-minute count format.

**Why:** Field-validated 2026-06-05 at Rocky Lake. **AM comp** ("One by One"): 19 fish in 30 minutes on TeleDragon 23' + 2 Lb mono + #2 hook + X-Series Slim Float + Maggots at 22". **PM practice (1 hr after the comp):** ran an A/B test with **identical tackle** on OmniFloat 14'10" SE (match rod) vs TeleValentine 23' (telescopic) — **OmniFloat was 2x slower per fish cycle**. The bottleneck is the rod-type cycle, NOT the line/hook/bait/float/depth/reel. OmniFloat got marginally more distant casts but the doubled cycle time made the extra reach uneconomic. Also confirmed in the PM block: **two panfish strike layers exist** at Rocky (12" and 22"), and **#4 hook works alongside #2**, on either mayflies OR maggots. 44 fish in the PM practice. Even at 19 comp catches, user was 30% behind top-4 leaders (40-50 catches) — the rig is correct; remaining gap is wait-time tuning + zone selection.

**Validated telescopic rigs (Rocky Lake panfish — two chassis confirmed):**
| Layer | Rod | Reel | Hook | Bait | Depth |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deep panfish band | **TeleDragon 23'** | Hydra 3000 | #2 | Maggots | **22"** |
| Shallow panfish band | **TeleValentine 23'** | Heartspinner 2500 | #4 | Mayflies (or maggots) | **12"** |

Line is **Mono .005" (~2 Lb)** on both rigs — deliberately under the rod rating. Float is **X-Series Slim Float** on the AM-validated rig (PM rig may use the same — confirm next session). Reels function as line spools; drag is largely irrelevant since landing is set + yank + swing.

**Run BOTH in parallel** during the next comp — covers both strike layers without rod swaps.

**Sequence tactic (validated 2026-06-05 3rd return):** Cast the TeleValentine (12" mayflies) FIRST at each spot as a "scout" — the shallow layer commits faster. If it hits, **immediately follow up the SAME spot** with the TeleDragon (22" maggots). **~50% hit rate on the follow-up within ~15 seconds.** Active panfish zones are STRATIFIED across both depth layers — a hit at 12" is a strong signal that 22" is also live at the same spot. If the TeleValentine hasn't hit by ~30 sec, the zone is cold — move on without burning a follow-up cast.

**Bite cadence at Rocky (panfish):** Hits at 15-20 sec, hooksets at 30-45 sec, rare after 1 minute. If no commit by 1 minute, recast.

**Match-rod penalty (PM 2026-06-05 A/B):** OmniFloat 14'10" SE with **identical tackle** (same line / hook / bait / float / depth) was **2x slower per cycle** than the telescopic. OmniFloat is the LIGHT match rod in inventory — any heavier match is even slower. **No match rod is competitive at Rocky 1pt/fish.**

**Over-rated landing capability:** Telescopic + 2 Lb mono landed a **3.1 lb Trophy White Sucker** — 1-3 lb over the line rating is achievable because the rod blank absorbs the load. There is no drag mechanism to fail; technique is set + yank + swing.

**How to apply:**
- **Identify the scoring format FIRST.** 1-point-per-fish → count optimization → telescopic. Weight-based or single-fish-size formats → match or spinning rod for selectivity.
- **Choose the densest fish layer for shallow drops.** At Rocky that's panfish (crappie, bluegill, golden shiner, white sucker) at ~22". Don't waste sport-comp time on unique fish that take 1.5-2.5 min to hit.
- **Insect/worm bait is mandatory** when the comp rules specify it (Rocky "One by One" = insect/worm only). Maggots are the high-cycle baseline; test mayflies, worms, hoppers for hit-rate differences.
- **Under-rate the line on purpose.** Match line to fish size (1-3 lb panfish), not rod rating. The rod just needs to bend.
- **Iterate on three variables next time** — wait time (cut to 20 sec?), depth (try 12-18" and 28-36"), hook size (try #4 or #6 for faster commits). The rod/reel/float/line stay fixed.

**Format contrast:**
- **1pt/fish sport comp (count format):** Telescopic + ultralight + shallow + insect bait → MAX bite cycle
- **Trout comp (weight/quality format):** Match rod (OmniFloat 14'10" SE) + barbless hook + mayflies → selectivity for trophy class
- **Salmon comp (mid-weight competition):** Loki 7'6" SE + Flat Spoon (see [[feedback_loki_salmonid_mvp]])
- **Monster-unique class:** Black Needle + heavy fluoro (see [[feedback_kaniq_monster_unique_tackle]])

These are FOUR distinct competition rig categories. Match the rig to the scoring math, not the species.
