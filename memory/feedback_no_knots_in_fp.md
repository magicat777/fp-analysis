**Rule:** Fishing Planet does NOT model fishing knots. Line-to-leader connections are abstracted away — you attach a leader in the inventory UI and the game treats it as connected. There is no knot strength, no knot failure mechanic, no choice of knot type.

**Why:** User corrected this 2026-05-19 after I added a "verify FG knot / PR bobbin" caution to a Kaji Nori shock-leader slot. Real-world saltwater knot guidance is irrelevant to FP and dilutes the actionable parts of the advice.

**How to apply:**
- **Never** recommend specific knot types (FG, PR bobbin, Albright, Bimini twist, Palomar, etc.)
- **Never** add "verify knot integrity" or "knot strength" as a risk factor
- **Do** recommend leader material, line class, and connection points conceptually (e.g., "add a fluoro shock leader between braid and lure")
- Real-world saltwater lore that's still useful for FP: line type (mono/braid/fluoro), line weight matching, leader abrasion resistance, hook size, retrieve mechanics
- Real-world saltwater lore that's NOT useful for FP: knot selection, line memory, line twist, swivel quality, hook-tying technique

**Related abstractions in FP:**
- No reel maintenance beyond a single "Durability" stat (no servicing, no lubrication choices)
- No rod cleaning / saltwater rinse mechanic
- Hook sharpness is implicit in hook type/size; no "sharpen hook" action
- Leader knots = absent, but leaders themselves DO have a stat and matter
