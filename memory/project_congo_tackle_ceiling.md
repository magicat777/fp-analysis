At Congo River (base level 75, DLC), the game's highest market tackle caps at ~97 lb spinning (Nile Chasseur 6'3" SE) and 93 lb casting / 88 lb X-Heavy casting (Silverthorn Stake 7'6"). Reel breakage is constant even on the 97 lb rod.

**MAJOR REVISION (2026-04-21):** **The tackle-ceiling hypothesis was wrong.** At the "One Long and One Short" contest, user landed **Trophy Nile Perch 257 lb** on the NileChasseur 6'3" SE + GiantSpin 9000 SE using a **three-way rig + Offset Hook + Ribbed Shad 6"**. That's 2.6× the rod's 97 lb rating. The same day, a Level 100 player caught a **Unique Nile Perch 453 lb** on the same setup  -  confirming the ceiling is at or near the fp-collective API's stated Nile Perch max of 205 kg / 452 lb.

**The RIG is the ceiling, not the rod.** Previously we assumed rod+reel line rating capped effective landed weight. The three-way rig changes this by:
- Suspending bait off the bottom in a natural hover zone (Nile Perch strike from below)
- Ribbed Shad 6" has inherent action even at rest
- Offset hook buried in soft plastic = weedless + solid hooksets
- Sinker dropper takes the shock load off the main line

**Silverthorn retired (2026-04-19):** Replaced by SharpCaster 7'3" + Rivertex Tornado 7500 in Slot 3. The 203 lb PB on Silverthorn has now been surpassed on the NileChasseur  -  so Silverthorn's Congo advantage was illusory; the right rig on a lighter rod outperforms the heaviest rod with the wrong rig.

**Why:** User asked a direct question  -  how do higher-level players fish this location given the tackle cap? The answer is: they don't use the market-heaviest rods; they use rigs that transfer load mechanics away from the line class.

**Update 2026-04-23 AM: FINESSE RETRIEVE IS MANDATORY.** User broke 2 reels trying to brute-force 100+ lb Nile Perch at max reel/tension. Reel wear rate is ~1%/sec at max settings  -  100 seconds until destruction. Landed the 4th big fish using finesse: line tension JUST UNDER fish's pull strength (some slack) + reel speed 1-2 only (low to low-medium). Goal: avoid triggering fish into maximum aggression where it runs hundreds of yards and destroys the reel. **Tackle weight does NOT save you**  -  lesser tackle also caused reel wear + broken lines. See `feedback_congo_finesse_retrieve.md` for full technique. **This retroactively explains how the 272 lb Kamba and 257 lb Perch were landed.**

**⚠ CRITICAL RIG CONSTRAINT:** Multi-leg rigs (Three-way, Texas, Carolina) only work on **Spinning and Casting rods**. They **cannot** be deployed on:
- Bottom rods (Humerus, Mokonzi, Tribal Totem)
- Feeder rods
- Carp rods
- Match rods (Brutus)

**Why this matters:** When recommending the three-way rig setup, only recommend it for spinning/casting slots. For bottom/feeder/carp/match rods, the recommendation should remain traditional sinker + leader + live/cut bait.
