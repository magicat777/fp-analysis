**USER-VALIDATED RULE (Everglades, contradicts forum consensus):**

> Trophy series CAN materially boost trophy/unique hit rate on the right SKU — confirmed for Trophy Bass Jig #4/0 at Everglades. ~10-20% better trophy largemouth rate vs non-Trophy alternatives. Two unique largemouth in the user's logs were caught ONLY on the Trophy #4/0, never on the standard #3/0.

**Contest rotation tactic (user-developed, Everglades Bass Challenge / Largemouth comps):**

1. **Open with Trophy #4/0 Bass Jig** — go after the hardest, biggest fish first (trophies + uniques). Slower base rate, but those catches are score-disproportionate.
2. **Switch down to standard #3/0 Bass Jig + Shad 3" trailer** once trophies are bagged — higher hit rate on regular largemouth, fills out the cast count for total weight/catch points.

This is the inverse of the standard "always use the highest-tier tackle" instinct. The Trophy variant has a narrower fish-class targeting profile; using it past the trophy-grab phase costs you bite frequency.

**Why this works:**
- Trophy items appear to bias toward trophy/unique fish at the cost of regular hit rate
- Standard items have broader appeal across the size class
- Contests reward bite frequency × catch size — rotation gets both

**How to apply:**

- When user has Trophy gear for a location, recommend it FIRST in contest-opening loadouts, then a non-Trophy fallback for fill.
- Do not assume Trophy is automatically better than standard — only for SKUs validated by user testing or community testing for that location/species.
- When recommending X-Series, push line/leader/hook upgrades hard; treat X-Series lures as optional logistics, not bite-rate buffs.
- Per dev statements: X-Series is 10-15% better "by slots, durability, and other stats" — NOT by fish attraction.
- **Club-Series ≈ X-Series tier**, purchased not earned. Recommend when single-SKU multi-condition coverage is the goal (e.g., Glow&Holo lures for day + low-light versatility). Do NOT recommend Club-Series expecting a bite-rate boost — the value is slot economy.
- **Ranking for contest-heavy / mission-completion play:** (1) Trophy first on validated SKUs, (2) X-Series line/leader/hooks/floats, (3) Club-Series when one SKU needs to span multiple conditions.
- **Honest gap:** Club-Series stat deltas are not publicly documented. To confirm specs, compare in-game shop side-by-side with non-Club equivalents.
