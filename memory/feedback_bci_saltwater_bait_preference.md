On 2026-04-26 user confirmed at Blue Crab Island that **saltwater baits outperform freshwater baits** for bite rate, even though both work.

**Saltwater bait winners at BCI:**
- **Sand Fleas**  -  saltwater specialist
- **Muscle Meat**  -  saltwater specialist
- **Finger Mullets**  -  saltwater specialist

**Freshwater baits at BCI:** Still produce, but slower bite rate. Use as fallback when saltwater bait inventory is low.

**Why:** BCI is a brackish/saltwater-influx location. The fish (Tarpon, Snook, Permit, Drum, Bonefish, etc.) are saltwater + brackish species  -  they recognize and respond to saltwater bait profiles more readily than freshwater equivalents.

**How to apply:**
- For BCI trip recommendations, ALWAYS include saltwater baits in the shopping list
- Default bait recommendations for BCI bottom rods: Sand Fleas / Muscle Meat / Finger Mullets
- Reserve freshwater baits (Shiners, Large Cutbait, Night Crawlers) as backup or for non-saltwater targets (Bowfin, Bullhead, Channel Catfish)
- This pattern likely applies to other future saltwater locations (Florida Keys, Skarland Fjord Norway, Maldives)  -  use saltwater baits primarily

**What NOT to recommend:**
- Don't tell user to use Night Crawlers as primary BCI bait  -  works but slow
- Don't generalize freshwater bait recipes from Saint-Croix/Dnipro to BCI  -  different water class, different fish preferences
