**Decision priority — in order:**

1. **Depth > rod length?** → **Slider only.** Round/pear/waggler are capped to leader length, which can't exceed rod length. Slider's ring lets line pass through, fishing past that ceiling.
2. **Casting beyond ~40 ft?** → **Waggler.** Aerodynamic, lays flat in flight, stands upright on water. Longest distance of any class.
3. **Light bites (panfish, roach, bluegill, skimmers)?** → **Thin pear / slim float.** Smallest tip dip on light bites.
4. **Big bait or current/chop?** → **Heavy waggler / chubby / loaded.** Fatter body resists drift; thin floats get swept (see Fairytale current note).
5. **Night?** → **Glowing variant** of whatever class you picked. Cosmetic; no mechanical change.
6. **Low-level rod?** → Bobber-socket only. Wagglers/sliders need match-class rods.

**Class capabilities:**

| Class | Distance | Depth | Stability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Round/Pear bobber | Shortest | Locked | Tilts if misset |
| Thin pear | Short-med | Locked | Sensitive tip |
| Waggler | Longest | Locked | Stays upright regardless |
| Slider | Med-long | Unlocked (ring passes line) | Tilts if misset |

**Where FP fudges it:** Forum testers report slider vs waggler bite rates are nearly identical when both can reach depth. The discriminating capability for slider is depth > rod length; for waggler it's casting distance. Otherwise interchangeable.

**Why:** Validated against past sessions — Congo Brutus + Glowing Pear for Tigerfish (sensitivity + night vis), Congo Wicked Viking + Waggler Heavy for Trophy Vundu 79 lb (mass to hold big bait in current), SJD MCT McCarp + Chubby for carp bottom-hold, Saint-Croix Fairytale current = float broken regardless of type.

**How to apply:** When recommending a float rig, walk the 6-step decision flow before naming a specific float. Don't default to round bobber out of habit — match-class rods unlock waggler/slider and those usually win at any distance > shallow shoreline.

**Inventory gap as of 2026-05-11:** 37 basic floats / 2 wagglers / 1 slider. Heavy on bobbers, light on the capable match attachments. Add mid-weight wagglers + at least one Heavy Slider + a Glowing Pear before any serious deep-water or SoAm match work.
