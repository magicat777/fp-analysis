On 2026-04-25 at Blue Crab Island, user ran two experimental loadout slots with rods designed for lighter fish classes:

- **Slot 2: UncleSam 6'3" Spinning (Heavy, 1/2-1 3/4 Oz lure, 6-21 Lb line)** + Patriot's Dream 4000  -  wore reel down to **27,226 / 46,200 (59%) durability**
- **Slot 5: LibertyCast 8'10" Casting (Med Light, 1/5-5/8 Oz lure, 3.5-10.5 Lb line)** + AmericanEagle 2000  -  wore reel down to **9,242 / 46,200 (20% RED  -  needs repair)** durability

Both reels suffered significant wear from **medium-big fish hitting undersized rod/reel combos**  -  even though user was not deliberately brute-forcing (which is the Congo finesse rule). The mismatch alone caused damage.

**Why:** When a rod's line rating or reel's drag is too low for the fish's pull strength, every fight stresses the reel mechanism beyond its sustained-load tolerance. Even with finesse retrieve technique, the cumulative wear is significant. This is separate from the maximum-drag reel-destruction issue at Congo (1%/sec  -  that's a brute-force problem). This is a **chronic underspec problem**.

**At Blue Crab Island specifically:** fish class includes Drum (40-100+ lb), Tarpon (up to 154 lb), Snook (up to 40 lb), Permit (up to 77 lb), Striped Bass (up to 55 lb). A 6-21 Lb-rated rod or 3.5-10.5 Lb-rated rod will get hit by these fish frequently  -  there's no way to "avoid" them. Each hit damages the reel.

**How to apply:**

1. **Match rod's line rating to expected fish class minimum:**
   - 5-15 lb fish → rod rated 6-15 Lb or 8-20 Lb
   - 15-50 lb fish → rod rated 15-30 Lb minimum (Brutus 11-34 Lb works)
   - 50-100 lb fish → rod rated 28-66 Lb minimum (SharpCaster 28.6-66 Lb works)
   - 100+ lb fish → rod rated 46+ Lb (NileChasseur 46-97 Lb)

2. **Don't slot DLC light rods (UncleSam, LibertyCast) at locations with medium-big fish.** They're fine at:
   - Rocky Lake (trout comp, sub-3 lb fish)
   - Tiber / Lesní Víla (panfish, sub-5 lb)
   - Lone Star Lake (smaller fish)
   - Mudwater River (mid-class)

3. **Recommended BCI Slot 2 + 5 replacements:**
   - Slot 5 LibertyCast → DragonCast 7'3" NE (Heavy, 7.5-23 Lb) + Basilisk 3500 P  -  same lure-weight range, proper line strength
   - Slot 2 UncleSam → either upgrade reel to a 33+ Lb drag class (VorteX 7000 SE, Cyclone 6500 SE) OR shelve and run 6 active rods

4. **Pre-trip check:** before any trip, verify each rod's line rating ≥ fish class minimum at the location. If not, shelve the rod or swap it.

5. **The Congo finesse rule applies AFTER rod/reel matching:** even with a properly-matched rod, you still need finesse retrieve at Congo for 100+ lb fish. But mismatched rods cause wear regardless of retrieve technique.
