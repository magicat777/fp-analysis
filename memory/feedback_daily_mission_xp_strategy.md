**Rule:** Daily missions in FP are **biased toward locations you have unlocked AND recently visited**. They are NOT random across all owned locations.

**Why:** The mission generator weights recently-visited unlocked locations heavily. User-validated 2026-05-17: every daily set since reaching the four endgame DLC locations (Skarland, Maldives, Kaji Nori, Congo) has drawn exclusively from those four — never asks for a fish at, say, Lone Star when the user's last trip was Skarland.

**Observed structure of a daily set:**
- **Difficulty split: 60% easy / 30% moderate / 10% difficult** (across the 9 catch tasks)
- **Location split: 6 / 2 / 1** — six tasks share one primary location, two share a secondary, one is at a tertiary
- **XP per catch task: 90,000–120,000 XP**
- **Max daily haul if all 9 completed: ~300,000–400,000 XP** (= multiple levels at high tiers)
- **Reset time: 2100 PST**

**How to apply:**
- **Stay parked at one DLC location** between resets — the next set will weight that location heavily (6 of 9 tasks)
- **Plan a 2-location rotation** if dailies bridge two trips: do 6/9 at the primary, then travel once to clear the 2/9 secondary, leaving the 1/9 tertiary as a stretch goal
- **Don't chase the difficult 10%** if it requires an inconvenient location swap — the 60% easy + 30% moderate already cover 90% of the XP at lower travel cost
- **The three mission categories (daily, club, premium) each draw from the same recent-location pool** — synergy means a club mission at Skarland often pairs with a daily at Skarland
- **Club bonus stacks**: when another club member is online at the SAME location, user gets a slight XP bump (additional reason to coordinate club nights at one DLC)

**Validated leveling pace using this strategy (2026-05-17):**
- User went from low-70s to level 83 in this push, "more than a dozen levels" via the four-DLC rotation
- Combined with trophy/unique first-catch bonuses on the same trips, leveling rate exceeds non-DLC baseline per [[feedback_skarland_ocean_pace]]
