In Fishing Planet competitions, **bite frequency beats individual fish size** for scoring. Top placers pull 30-40 fish in a window while mid-pack players land 6-9.

Observed at two competitions:
- **SJD Night Sturgeon (2026-04-18 approx):** User landed 6 sturgeon in 40 min on bottom rigs + Large Cutbait. Top competitors hit 30-40 sturgeon in the same window  -  likely smaller hooks + faster-bite bait (Crawfish, Corn, or Pellets instead of Large Cutbait).
- **Rocky Lake Old Buck's Comp (2026-04-19):** User placed 49 with 9 fish / 17.573 lb on 1/2 Oz barbless spinner on SharpCaster. Switching to 1/5 Oz #1/0 nano-spinner produced more hookups  -  but the X-Heavy SharpCaster (5/8-2 Oz rated) couldn't cast the 1/5 Oz lure far enough to reach target zones.

**Why:**
- Competition scoring rewards catch count, not monster fish
- Smaller/barbless hooks penetrate faster on the softer-jawed smaller fish
- Faster-retrieve reactive lures get more investigative strikes than big meat bait

**How to apply:**
- For any FP competition, default to **smaller hooks** (#1/0 to #3/0) and **smaller lures** (1/5 - 1/2 Oz for trout, Crawfish/Corn/Pellets for sturgeon)
- Always bring a **rod matched to the lure weight**  -  the X-Heavy SharpCaster can't cast 1/5 Oz effectively; need a Light-class rod like JigWinner 8'10" SE (1/6-5/8 Oz rated)
- **Before a comp**, check the rod lure-weight range against the lures you plan to use. Mismatch = no casting distance = locked out of the target zone
- Individual trophy fish are **bonus, not the goal** in competitions  -  consistency wins
- User's validated plan: JigWinner 8'10" SE + Prima 3500 SE (11 Lb drag, 5.2:1) + 1/5 Oz barbless spinner for next trout comp

**Stopwatch protocol — time-between-hits is a measurable variable:**

Bring a stopwatch (mental or actual) and recast if no hit by the fish-class wait-time target. Sitting too long on a dead cast is the biggest invisible cost in count-format comps.

| Fish class | Target wait-to-hit | Recast trigger | Example contexts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Light to medium** (panfish, trout, small bass) | **15-30 sec** | Recast at 30 sec if no nibble | Rocky 1pt/fish, BCI inshore, freshwater trout comps |
| **Medium to heavy** (catfish, big bass, salmon, large pike) | **30 sec - 1 min** | Recast at 1 min | Kaniq trophy salmon, Saint-Croix catfish, Congo mid-tier |
| **Extra heavy bottom** (deep ocean, sharks, halibut) | **1-3 min** | Hold the rod longer; deeper fish commit slower | **Norway/Skarland Fjord**, Maldives big-game bottom |

**Why this works:**
- Quantity-scored comps punish dead-cast time linearly — every minute on a non-producing cast is a minute of count opportunity lost
- The wait-time band tells you whether you're fishing the right LAYER, the right SPOT, or with the right BAIT — if you blow past the target window consistently, change one of those, don't just keep waiting
- For heavy bottom, the inverse applies — recasting too fast walks the bait out of the fish's investigation window before they commit

**How to validate at Rocky 6/5 data:** Panfish hit at 15-20 sec / hooked at 30-45 sec on the TeleDragon — squarely in the light/medium 15-30s target band. The 30% leaderboard gap behind top-4 is consistent with top players cutting recasts at 20 sec while user was holding to 45 sec.
